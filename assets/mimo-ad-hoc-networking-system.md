## Overview

This project focuses on the research and development of MIMO ad hoc networking systems for reliable, scalable, and self-organizing wireless connectivity.

My work covered the design and prototyping of physical-layer, link-layer, and network-layer algorithms, together with simulation, SDR/FPGA-based verification, and outdoor multi-node field experiments.

## My Contributions

* Designed and verified physical-layer frame structures, waveform structures, and transmit/receive beamforming algorithms.
* Designed MAC and link-layer frame structures to improve throughput, reliability, and scalability.
* Developed topology-aware routing mechanisms for multi-hop self-organizing connectivity.
* Built simulation platforms based on Wireless InSite, MATLAB, and Simulink for channel modeling, algorithm prototyping, and performance evaluation.
* Implemented and verified baseband algorithms on an SDR/FPGA platform.
* Conducted outdoor field experiments with multi-node mobility and analyzed experimental data for iterative system improvement.

## Keywords

MIMO ad hoc networks, self-organizing networks, beamforming, SDR/FPGA, MATLAB/Simulink, field experiments.
