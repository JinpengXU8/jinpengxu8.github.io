Hi! I am Jinpeng Xu, a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I received my Ph.D. from Beihang University (BUAA) in January 2025, under the supervision of Prof. Lin Bai and Prof. Lin Zhou. During my doctoral studies, I also benefited greatly from the guidance and mentorship of Prof. Xiang Cheng at Peking University. Prior to joining HKUST, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU).

My research focuses on developing efficient, robust, secure, and intelligent low-altitude wireless networks. I work on key theoretical and technical problems in channel modeling, beamforming, signal processing and optimization, physical-layer security, and integrated sensing and communication, with an interest in AI-enabled network optimization and autonomous wireless networking. My research is closely related to MIMO ad-hoc networks, UAV/low-altitude wireless networks, and space-air-ground integrated networks.
My work combines theoretical modeling, simulation, algorithm design, and system-oriented validation, with experience in USRP/SDR-based prototyping, ARM/FPGA-based implementation, and real-world field experiments.

## Research Interests

- Channel modeling, signal processing, beamforming, and optimization
- Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC)
- Physical-layer security (PLS)
- MIMO ad-hoc networks
- Low-altitude wireless networks (LAWNs)
- Space-air-ground integrated networks (SAGINs)
- AI for networks
