Hi! I am Jinpeng Xu, a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I received my Ph.D. from Beihang University (BUAA) in January 2025, under the supervision of Prof. Lin Bai and Prof. Lin Zhou. During my doctoral studies, I also benefited greatly from the guidance and mentorship of Prof. Xiang Cheng at Peking University. Prior to joining HKUST, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). My selected honors include the Outstanding Graduate Award from Beihang University and a nomination for Beihang University’s Top Ten Graduate Students Award. I was also a member of the teams that received the First Prize of the Science and Technology Progress Award from the Chinese Institute of Command and Control and the Second Prize of the Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Progress Award.

My research focuses on the development of efficient, robust, secure, and intelligent low-altitude wireless networks. I investigate fundamental theoretical and technical challenges in channel modeling, beamforming, signal processing and optimization, physical-layer security, and integrated sensing and communications, with particular interests in AI-enabled network optimization and autonomous wireless networking. My research spans MIMO ad hoc networks, UAV-enabled and low-altitude wireless networks, and space-air-ground integrated networks. My work integrates theoretical modeling, simulation, algorithm design, and system-level validation, supported by hands-on experience in USRP/SDR-based prototyping, ARM/FPGA implementation, and field experiments in real-world environments.

## Research Interests

- Channel modeling, signal processing, beamforming, and optimization
- Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC)
- Physical-layer security (PLS)
- MIMO ad-hoc networks
- Low-altitude wireless networks (LAWNs)
- Space-air-ground integrated networks (SAGINs)
- AI for networks
